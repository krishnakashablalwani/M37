PART 1: PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)1. Product Vision & ScopeA B2B multi-distributor ordering, replenishment, and distribution management platform for Pharmaceutical, Surgical, and FMCG supply chains. It provides a "Zomato-like" consumer-grade ordering experience for retailers while enforcing strict B2B pharmaceutical compliance (Drug License verification, Schedule H/H1/X restrictions, batch/expiry tracking, and GST-compliant distributor invoicing).Core Value PropositionsFor Retailers: 1-tap automated replenishment, cross-distributor price/scheme comparison, real-time stock visibility, and centralized GST invoice & ledger tracking.For Distributors: Automated order intake, batch-level FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) inventory reservation, zero-overselling protection, and credit/receivables management.For Admins: Centralized Master SKU Catalog (eliminating duplicate product naming across stockists), territory/mapping control, and platform-wide auditability.2. Functional Requirements & User StoriesEpic 1: Compliance-First Onboarding & KYCFR-1.1: System must require GSTIN and State Drug License Numbers (Form 20/21 for Retailers; 20B/21B for Distributors) if the entity opts into Pharmaceutical categories. FMCG-only retailers only require GSTIN/Udyam registration.FR-1.2: Restricted Category Guardrail: Retailers without a verified, non-expired Drug License on file must be blocked at the API and UI level from adding Schedule H, H1, or X products to their cart.FR-1.3: Drug License expiry cron job must alert retailers 30 days prior to expiry and automatically restrict pharma ordering upon expiration until renewed documents are approved.Epic 2: Master Product Catalog & Distributor Inventory MappingFR-2.1 (Single Source of Truth): Admin maintains a canonical master_products table (Brand Name, Generic/Salt Composition, Category [Pharma/Surgical/FMCG], Schedule Type, Pack Size, HSN Code, GST %, Barcode).FR-2.2 (Distributor Inventory Layer): Distributors do not create duplicate product listings; they map their stock to a master_product_id and maintain batch-level records (batch_number, expiry_date, available_qty, reserved_qty, ptr [Price to Retailer], mrp, scheme_text e.g., "10+1 Free", moq).FR-2.3 (Bulk CSV/Excel Ingestion): Distributors can upload daily stock/price sheets via CSV/Excel. A staging parser validates SKUs, flags unrecognized product names for fuzzy-matching against the Master Catalog, and outputs an error report CSV for failed rows.Epic 3: Smart Search & Zomato-Style DiscoveryFR-3.1: Instant debounced search (<150ms) across Brand Name, Generic Salt Name (e.g., searching "Paracetamol 650" shows all brands), Manufacturer, SKU Code, and Barcode.FR-3.2 (Substitute & Comparison Drawer): On any Product Detail Page, display all eligible distributors serving the retailer's pincode in a comparison card showing:Net Landing Price (PTR + GST − Discount/Scheme value)Available Quantity & Earliest Batch Expiry DateExpected Delivery SLA (e.g., "Next Day Morning")Direct "Call Distributor" button (role-gated to verified retailers).Epic 4: Automated Order Generation Engine (Core Differentiator)FR-4.1 (Data Source Transparency): Each auto-generated line item must explicitly carry a badge: Based on Verified POS Sales (if retailer uploads/syncs stock) or Estimated from Order History (if calculated from platform purchase velocity).FR-4.2 (Replenishment Formula):$$\text{Suggested Qty} = \max\left(0, \text{Target Stock} - (\text{Current Stock} + \text{In-Transit Qty})\right)$$Where:$$\text{Target Stock} = \text{Avg Daily Demand}_{(7/15/30\text{d})} \times (\text{Lead Time Days} + \text{Safety Buffer Days})$$The engine automatically rounds up to the nearest pack multiple and enforces the distributor's Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and shelf-life threshold (e.g., ignore distributor batches expiring within 90 days unless flagged as clearance).FR-4.3 (Non-Autonomous Guardrail): Auto-generated orders land in the SUGGESTED_DRAFT state at a scheduled time (e.g., 8:00 PM daily). Retailers receive a push/WhatsApp notification to review, edit quantities, and tap "Confirm Order". Unconfirmed drafts never trigger distributor dispatch unless the admin and retailer explicitly sign off on an auto-confirm SLA.Epic 5: Intelligent Distributor Routing & Order SplittingFR-5.1 (Mode A — Smart Allocation): When an order is generated or placed in Smart Mode, the routing engine scores eligible distributors using configurable admin weights:$$\text{Score} = w_1(\text{Preferred Status}) + w_2(\text{Fill Rate \%}) + w_3(\text{Price/Scheme Competitiveness}) + w_4(\text{Delivery SLA}) + w_5(\text{Historical Reliability})$$FR-5.2 (Shortfall & Split Handling): If the primary distributor has only 40 out of 100 requested units:The UI prompts the retailer BEFORE order confirmation with two clear options: Split Order (40 from Distributor A, 60 from Best Alternative Distributor B) or Switch Whole Line Item to Distributor B.Strict No-Silent-Rerouting Rule: Once a retailer confirms an order with Distributor A, the system is strictly prohibited from silently reassigning the order to Distributor B if Distributor A rejects or short-supplies it. Instead, the rejected/short items transition to SHORTFALL_ACTION_REQUIRED, prompting the retailer with a 1-tap "Re-order shortfall from [Alternative Distributor]" action.Epic 6: Order Lifecycle, FEFO Stock Reservation & GST InvoicingFR-6.1 (State Machine):SUGGESTED_DRAFT → AWAITING_RETAILER_APPROVAL → ORDER_PLACED → AWAITING_DISTRIBUTOR_CONFIRMATION → ACCEPTED / PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED / REJECTED → PROCESSING → PACKED → DISPATCHED → OUT_FOR_DELIVERY → DELIVERED → RETURN_REQUESTED → RETURNED_PARTIAL / RETURNED_FULL.FR-6.2 (Concurrency & Atomic Reservation): Accepting an order atomically decrements available_qty and increments reserved_qty on the earliest-expiring eligible batch (FEFO). Simultaneous checkouts cannot double-book the last remaining units.FR-6.3 (Legal Invoicing Separation): Invoices are legally generated on behalf of the fulfilling Distributor (displaying the Distributor's Legal Name, Address, Drug License Nos., and GSTIN as the Seller, and Retailer's details as Buyer)—never the platform entity.FR-6.4: Invoice PDF includes HSN code, Batch No., Expiry Date, MRP, PTR, Scheme/Discount %, CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown, and Total Payable.Epic 7: Payments, Credit Ledgers & Returns (RMA)FR-7.1 (Decoupled Financial States): Order Status (DELIVERED), Invoice Status (ISSUED), and Payment Status (UNPAID / PARTIALLY_PAID / PAID / OVERDUE) are tracked as three orthogonal state dimensions.FR-7.2 (Credit Limit & Aging Control): Distributors can configure a credit_limit and credit_days (e.g., ₹50,000 / 15 days) per retailer. Orders exceeding credit limits warn the retailer to pay via UPI/Gateway or await distributor credit override.FR-7.3 (Returns & Credit Notes): Retailers submit RMA requests tied to a specific invoice_line_item_id and batch_number with mandatory photo evidence for damages/shortages. Upon distributor approval, stock is logged into quarantine_damaged_stock or salable_stock (with audit trail) and a GST-compliant Credit Note automatically adjusts the retailer's outstanding balance.
